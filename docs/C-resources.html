C.2 Free courses and tutorials worth reading next

These are other people’s teaching materials. They explain some topics from different angles and with different data, and are well worth reading alongside this book.

  • Gábor Mészáros, Genomics Boot Camp: a livestock-focused introduction to PLINK and R, with video lessons.
  • Matti Pirinen, GWAS course (University of Helsinki): the statistics behind GWAS, including relatedness, structure, LD, meta-analysis and imputation, with R code.
  • Marees and colleagues, GWA tutorial: scripts that go with their 2018 paper on QC and association in human data.
  • Nicola Barban and colleagues, Sociogenomics summer school 2023.

C.5 A note on originality

The explanations, worked examples, numbers and figures in this book were written and produced for it, using its own simulated and public data. Standard formulas (Hardy-Weinberg, F, D and r², the GRM, KING, λ, FST) and standard cut-offs (such as the KING relationship categories) are given with their original references. Where a teaching idea is shared with the courses listed above, those courses are acknowledged here and in the Welcome page.
