19  A complete QC pipeline and the answer key

This chapter puts everything together: one script that turns the raw practice file (361 animals, 4,800 SNPs) into the clean file used from Chapter 12 onwards (351 animals, 4,443 SNPs), followed by the answer key to the hidden problems.

19.1 The pipeline at a glance

flowchart TD
  A["Raw data<br/>361 animals, 4,800 SNPs"] --> B["0. Look<br/>--missing, --freq, --hardy within breed,<br/>--test-missing, --het, --check-sex,<br/>--mendel, --genome, PCA"]
  B --> C["1. Decide<br/>animals to remove, sexes to fix,<br/>SNPs to exclude, breed labels to fix"]
  C --> D["2a. Animals<br/>--remove, --update-sex"]
  D --> E["2b. SNPs<br/>--exclude, --geno 0.05"]
  E --> F["2c. Safety check<br/>--mind 0.05"]
  F --> G["2d. --maf 0.01"]
  G --> H["Clean data<br/>351 animals, 4,443 SNPs"]
  H --> I["3. Structure for association<br/>prune, PCA"]

19.2 The script

# 0. Convert and look (nothing is removed yet)
plink --file cattle --cow --make-bed --out cattle
plink --bfile cattle --cow --missing --out miss                                # Chapter 6
plink --bfile cattle --cow --freq --out freq                                   # Chapter 7
for b in ANG HOL NDA; do                                                       # Chapter 7: HWE within breed
  plink --bfile cattle --cow --keep k_$b.txt --hardy --out hwe_$b
done
plink --bfile cattle --cow --allow-no-sex --test-missing --out tmiss           # Chapter 6
plink --bfile cattle --cow --autosome --indep-pairwise 50 5 0.2 --out prune
plink --bfile cattle --cow --extract prune.prune.in --het --out het            # Chapter 8
plink --bfile cattle --cow --check-sex --out sexcheck                          # Chapter 8
plink --bfile cattle --cow --mendel --out mendel                               # Chapter 8
plink --bfile cattle --cow --extract prune.prune.in --genome --min 0.2 --out ibd   # Chapter 10
plink --bfile cattle --cow --extract prune.prune.in --maf 0.05 --pca 10 header tabs --out pca  # Chapter 11

# 1. Build the lists from those results (by reading the outputs and plots)
#    remove_animals.txt : 6 poor-DNA animals, contaminated ANG_030, duplicate HOL_050_rep,
#                         calf with the wrong sire HOL_F03_C2, crossbred HOL_077
#    sex_update.txt     : 4 animals set to their genetic sex
#    snp_exclude.txt    : 12 SNPs failing HWE (p < 1e-6) in any breed
#                         + 8 SNPs with --test-missing p < 1e-4
#    breed_fixed.clst   : ANG_066 relabelled as HOL

# 2. Apply them: animals first, then SNPs
plink --bfile cattle --cow --remove remove_animals.txt --update-sex sex_update.txt --make-bed --out step1
plink --bfile step1  --cow --exclude snp_exclude.txt --geno 0.05 --make-bed --out step2
plink --bfile step2  --cow --mind 0.05 --make-bed --out step3
plink --bfile step3  --cow --maf 0.01 --make-bed --out cattle_qc

# 3. Structure for the association models
plink --bfile cattle_qc --cow --autosome --indep-pairwise 50 5 0.2 --out qc_prune
plink --bfile cattle_qc --cow --extract qc_prune.prune.in --pca 10 header tabs --out qc_pca

What the logs reported at each step:

Step Log line Animals SNPs
start 361 4,800
step1 --update-sex: 4 cattle updated. --remove: 351 cattle remaining. 351 4,800
step2 --exclude: 4780 variants remaining. then 25 variants removed due to missing genotype data (--geno). 351 4,755
step3 0 cattle removed due to missing genotype data (--mind). 351 4,755
cattle_qc 312 variants removed due to minor allele threshold(s) 351 4,443

Why the order: decisions about animals come first, so a few poor samples cannot push good SNPs over the missingness cut-off (Chapter 6). The lists come from within-breed statistics, so breed differences do not cause false alarms (Chapters 7 and 8). --mind is repeated after the SNP filters as a safety check; removing nobody is the expected result.

TipKeep the lists

The files remove_animals.txt, sex_update.txt and snp_exclude.txt are your QC record. Put them in the repository with your scripts, and report the numbers removed at each step in your methods section.

19.3 Answer key: the hidden problems and how PLINK found them

Hidden problem Found by What PLINK showed
25 SNPs with high missingness --missing, --geno 0.05 All 25 above 2% missing (Chapter 6)
6 animals with poor DNA (ANG_012, ANG_045, HOL_023, NDA_017, NDA_061, HOL_F07_C1) --missing, --mind 6.8% to 24.6% missing; the next animal only 0.8% (Chapter 6)
8 SNPs failing more often in cases --test-missing Exactly the top 8, p = 3.3 × 10⁻⁹ to 4.9 × 10⁻⁵ (Chapter 6)
12 SNPs with heterozygote-calling errors --hardy within breed All 12 at p < 10⁻⁶ within breed; pooling the breeds flagged 114 (Chapter 7)
4 SNPs with random genotype errors --mendel Only 2 to 5 Mendel errors each: not caught, which is realistic for 30 families (Chapter 8)
Sex errors ANG_071, NDA_022 (bulls recorded as cows) --check-sex F on X = 1 with recorded sex 2 (Chapter 8)
Missing sex HOL_064, NDA_009 --check-sex SNPSEX = 1 (Chapter 8)
Wrong sire for HOL_F03_C2 (true sire HOL_011) --mendel, --genome 534 Mendel errors; PI_HAT 0 with the recorded sire and Z1 = 0.96 with HOL_011 (Chapters 8 and 10)
Contaminated DNA in ANG_030 --het, --genome F = −0.72; looks related to almost everyone (Chapters 8 and 10)
Duplicate sample HOL_050 / HOL_050_rep --genome PI_HAT 0.98 (Chapter 10); also pulled PC3 in the founders’ PCA (Chapter 11)
Inbred animal NDA_040 --het, --homozyg F = 0.43; F_ROH 0.29 (Chapters 8 and 10)
Crossbred HOL_077 (half N’Dama) PCA Halfway between Holstein and N’Dama (Chapter 11)
Mislabelled ANG_066 (really Holstein) PCA, --cluster --K 3 Inside the Holstein cloud and cluster (Chapter 11)
40 strand-flipped SNPs in batch 2 --bmerge error, --flip 39 caught; the C/G SNP snp03921 was invisible (Chapter 5)
BRD risk SNP snp00706 --logistic with PCs, --mh --within Rank 238 naive, rank 1 after correction, p = 2.3 × 10⁻⁸ (Chapter 12)
Weaning-weight QTL snp02145 (PLAG1 region, +9 kg) --linear with covariates, --dosage, meta-analysis p = 0.93 naive, 9.7 × 10⁻¹¹ with covariates; 9.6 kg estimated (Chapter 13)
Weaning-weight QTL snp00891 (LCORL region, +5 kg) --linear p = 0.0019, rank 18: real but underpowered (Chapter 13)
Feedlot-only effect snp01540 (+12 kg in feedlot) --linear --interaction ADD × FEEDLOT = −10.5 kg, p = 6.3 × 10⁻⁵ (Chapter 13)
Epistatic pair for BRD (snp00210 × snp03131) --epistasis Not detected (p = 0.57): almost every animal carried the risk combination (Chapter 16)
Deletion hotspot on chr 7 near 40 Mb, and higher CNV burden in cases CNV --mperm, --cnv-indiv-perm 34 cases against 6 controls, EMP2 = 0.0001; 4.30 against 2.95 CNVs per animal (Chapter 15)

Almost every planted problem was found, and the two that were not (four SNPs with a handful of random errors, and the weak epistatic pair) are the kind real studies also miss. That is the honest lesson of QC: it catches the big problems reliably and the small ones only sometimes, which is why replication matters.

19.4 Reproduce everything

All data, scripts and figures are in the GitHub repository:

Folder or file Contents
data/ The simulated cattle (cattle.ped/.map, batch2, pheno.txt, covar.txt, breed.clst, genes.txt, chr14.dosage, cattle.cnv, TRUTH.json) and data/real/ (the real cattle and sheep)
scripts/simulate.py Makes the whole simulated dataset (seed 2026)
scripts/chapters/ The PLINK commands of every chapter, one script per chapter
qc_lists/ The QC lists used to build cattle_qc (removed animals, sex updates, excluded SNPs, PCs)
scripts/convert_real_data.py Converts the real cattle data from the synbreedData package to PLINK format
scripts/make_figures.py Draws every figure from the PLINK output
downloads/plink_tutorial_data_and_scripts.zip Everything above in one file

The results were produced with PLINK v1.07, v1.9.2-dev and v2.0.0-b.1.5-dev built from the official source. Another build or random seed can give slightly different numbers.
